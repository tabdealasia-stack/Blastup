Frequently Asked Questions

What is minimum KM billing?
For outstation trips, a minimum billing of 300 KMs per day is applied. If your actual travel is less than 300 KMs, you will still be billed for 300 KMs. If it exceeds, billing is done on actual KMs.
Are tolls and parking included?
No, base fares do not include Tolls, Parking, or State Border Taxes. These are to be paid by the customer at actuals during the trip.
Can I book one-way travel?
Yes, we provide dedicated one-way drops to major cities like Pune, Mumbai, and Hyderabad. Please select the 'One Way Drop' option in the booking form for a specialized quote.
Are the drivers verified?
Absolutely. All our drivers hold valid commercial licenses, have their backgrounds verified, and carry years of experience on outstation highways.

Terms & Extras

Important Booking Information

Tolls, Parking, and State Border Taxes are extra and to be paid by the customer directly.

Kilometers and Time calculate from Garage to Garage.

A standard advance payment is required to confirm bookings.

Driver Assignment Cancellation: Once a driver has been assigned to the booking, any cancellation by the customer will attract applicable cancellation charges.

Excessive Engine Idling: To prevent unnecessary fuel consumption and engine wear, vehicles must not be left idle with the engine running for prolonged periods. Additional charges may apply for excessive idling.

Vehicle Care & Cleanliness: The vehicle will be provided in a clean and well-maintained condition. Customers are expected to return it in a reasonably clean state. If the interior is found excessively dirty or requires professional cleaning after the trip, a ₹500 cleaning fee will be charged.
